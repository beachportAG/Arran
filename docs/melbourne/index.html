Buying in Melbourne

Why Melbourne owners call us

Arran grew up in Melbourne, so this isn't a market he studied from a spreadsheet. He knows which streets flood, which roofs the carriers stopped writing, and which neighborhoods hold value when the rest of the coast wobbles. Melbourne has a wide age range of housing, from mid-century blocks near the older commercial corridors to newer construction west of I-95. The older stock carries the coastal wear you'd expect: corroded fasteners and fixtures, aging roofs, screen enclosures damaged in storms and never rebuilt, and windows that would never pass a current impact standard.

Insurance is the recurring obstacle. Carriers have grown selective about roof age and window ratings on this coast, and once coverage is refused a financed buyer can't close. That's the situation where a cash purchase is genuinely the practical answer rather than just the fast one.

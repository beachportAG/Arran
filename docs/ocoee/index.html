Buying in Ocoee

Why Ocoee owners call us

A large share of Ocoee was built between 1975 and 1998, which means an enormous number of homes are all arriving at the same maintenance cliff simultaneously. Original roofs long past their second replacement, HVAC systems on their last legs, polybutylene plumbing that insurers refuse to write, and kitchens that read as dated to every buyer walking through.

Fixing all of that before listing can cost forty or fifty thousand dollars that the owner doesn't have sitting around. We buy the house at its current condition so nobody has to finance a renovation just to sell.

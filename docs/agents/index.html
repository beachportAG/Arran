Why Agents Work With Us

You get paid twice, not zero times

A listing that sits for six months and expires pays you nothing. This is the alternative.

Full Commission, Paid at Closing

We pay a full commission through your brokerage at closing, the same as any buyer would. You represent your seller, we buy the house, everyone gets paid.

You Get the Relist

This is the part other investors won't offer. When the renovation is done and the property goes back on the market, it goes back to you. One property, two commissions.

No Financing to Fall Through

We buy with our own funds. No loan, no appraisal contingency, no underwriter killing your deal two weeks before closing and putting you back at square one.

Your Seller Stops Suffering

No more showings on a house that shows badly, no more price reductions, no more difficult conversations. Their problem ends on a date they choose.

We Close on Your Timeline

Seven days or ninety. If your seller needs to coordinate with a purchase on the other side, we'll work around it rather than dictating terms.

Straight Answer, Same Day

You'll know quickly whether we're interested and roughly where we'd land. A fast no is worth more to you than a slow maybe, so we don't sit on submissions to keep our options open.

What We're Looking For

Send us these

Good Fit

  • Single family, townhome, duplex, or small multi-family
  • Anywhere in Orange, Seminole, Osceola, Lake, Polk, or Brevard
  • Needs significant repair or has failed inspection
  • Expired or withdrawn after sitting on market
  • Seller needs speed or certainty over top dollar
  • Probate, estate, or out-of-state owner
  • Occupied by tenants, including non-paying tenants
  • Liens, code violations, or title complications
  • Pre-foreclosure with a sale date approaching

Not a Fit

  • Move-in ready homes priced at full retail
  • Properties far outside Central Florida
  • Sellers who need above-market pricing to make it work
  • Land with no structure or no clear path to develop
  • Large commercial or industrial buildings
  • Anything where listing clearly nets the seller more

The Process

Four steps, no surprises

01

Send the Address

Use the form below or just text it. Photos help but aren't required. We'll tell you quickly if it's not a fit rather than stringing you along.

02

We Walk It

Arran inspects the property himself, usually within 48 hours. Coordinate access however works for your seller.

03

Written Offer

You get a written offer with the commission stated in it. Present it to your seller like any other offer.

04

Close & Get Paid

Reputable title company, seller's chosen date, commission paid to your brokerage at closing. Then we call you for the relist.

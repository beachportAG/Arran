Buy Box

What we take, what we don't

Check this before you send. It saves us both a round trip.

We Buy

  • Single family, townhomes, duplexes, small multi-family
  • Orange, Seminole, Osceola, Lake, Polk, and Brevard counties
  • Any condition, including full gut and fire damage
  • Occupied properties, including non-paying tenants
  • Liens, code violations, and title issues
  • Probate and estate properties
  • Pre-foreclosure with a live sale date
  • Assignments and double closes both work

We Pass On

  • Deals already shopped to every buyer in Florida
  • Numbers built on an unrealistic after-repair value
  • Vacant land with no clear development path
  • Mobile homes on leased land
  • Properties outside our six counties
  • Deals where you don't actually control the contract
  • Large commercial or industrial
